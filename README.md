# muslim-mp-representation
Effective Representation of Muslim MPs in the Indian Parliament

Authors: Archit Das and Shalini Bhaiya
Course: Data Science for Social Science
Instructor: Professor Anushtubh Agnihotri
Date: May 07, 2025

Overview
This repository contains a research paper examining the institutional determinants of parliamentary participation by Muslim Members of Parliament (MPs) in India between 1999 and 2019. The study focuses on participation during Question Hour in the Lok Sabha and investigates how political and institutional conditions shape minority legislative engagement.

Research Focus
The study examines how different democratic, institutional, and exclusionary factors influence the frequency with which Muslim MPs ask questions in Parliament. Question Hour participation is used as the main quantitative indicator of parliamentary engagement.

Data Sources
The research relies on the following data sources as described in the paper:
- TCPD Indian Parliament Dataset (Question Hour)
- Varieties of Democracy (V-Dem) institutional indicators
- A manually coded list identifying Muslim MPs
This repository does not include these datasets.

Methods
The paper uses regression and residualization techniques to evaluate the association between institutional indicators and Muslim MP question-asking behavior. Several control variables are used in the analysis, including ruling coalition alignment, national election timing, non-Muslim MP activity, and state-year averages in question-asking.

Empirical Components
The paper includes:
- national comparison of question-asking patterns between Muslim and Non-Muslim MPs over time
- state-level comparison of parliamentary activity (Tamil Nadu vs. Uttar Pradesh)
- regression estimates of institutional predictors of Muslim MP participation
- residualized scatterplot visualizations of partial relationships between institutional indicators and participation
